      Get told when a clinic opens up

      Waitlists move without warning. Tell us what you need and we will email you when a clinic within your travel distance confirms an opening — no more calling ten clinics a month to ask. We skip clinics that have told us they don't take your insurance.

      One email per matching opening, at most one a week. Unsubscribe in a click. We never sell your information, and we never share it with clinics unless you contact them yourself.

      How this list stays honest

      • Dated, not assumed. Every entry shows when we last confirmed it, in plain language.
      • Openings expire. After 30 days an answer stops counting as current; after 90 we drop the claim entirely and ask again.
      • Empty beats wrong. If we have not asked a city yet, this page says so instead of guessing.
      • Clinics cannot buy their way onto this list. Featured placement is labeled and sold separately; it never changes whether a clinic appears here.
