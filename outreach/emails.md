# Outreach sequence — founding featured slots

**Do not bulk-send.** These go out in small batches, personalized, from a real mailbox, after
the recipient's profile is live and verified. CAN-SPAM applies: include a physical mailing
address and a working opt-out in every commercial email, and honor opt-outs immediately.
Never email a clinic whose profile has a data error you haven't fixed first.

Sequence: **E1 (value, no ask) → E2 four days later (the offer) → E3 seven days later (close or drop)**.
Anyone who replies leaves the sequence. Anyone who says no is marked `declined` in
prospects.csv and never emailed again.

## Physician-referral mailer (the 42% channel — separate track)

Print one-pagers per metro: current waitlist map of that city, ClearPath URL, no sales copy.
Mail to developmental pediatricians, ECI/Part C coordinators, and school district diagnosticians.
They hand it to families at diagnosis, which is the moment that matters. Track with a per-metro
short URL. This is the highest-value channel in the plan and costs postage.
