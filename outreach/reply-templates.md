# Reply templates

## "How do I know families actually use this?"

Straight answer: it launched recently, so I'm not going to quote you traffic I don't have. Here's
what's real — 1,379 Texas ABA organizations indexed, 467 with a license verified against the TDLR
roster, and pages built for the two searches families actually run: "ABA therapy in {{city}}" and
"ABA in {{city}} that accepts {{plan}}."

That's why founding pricing is $199 instead of $399 and why it's locked. You're taking the risk
that traffic is early; I'm taking the risk that I priced it too low once it isn't. If it doesn't
produce, cancel — no contract.

## "Can I just pay to rank first?"

No, and that's the point of the site. Featured slots sit in a labeled block at the top, capped at
three per city. The regular directory sorts by confirmed availability, then verified license,
then alphabetically. If paying moved you up the real list, families would stop trusting the list,
and then the slot would be worth nothing to you.

## "We're full / our waitlist is closed."

Then don't buy a slot — tell me your wait and I'll put it on your free listing. Families filter
for who's accepting, so an honest "closed" saves you intake calls you can't serve. Ping me when
you open up and I'll re-verify same week.

## "Take our listing down."

I'll remove the profile within one business day, no argument. (Worth knowing first: the
underlying records are public — federal NPI registry and the state license roster — so removal
takes you out of ABA Openings, not out of the public record. Most clinics would rather correct the
page than vanish from a list families are reading. Your call either way.)

## "Something on our page is wrong."

Send me the correction and I'll fix it today and re-date the stamp. If it's the license match,
it's usually a name variant between your NPI record's authorized official and the TDLR roster —
give me the director's name as it appears on the license and I'll match it manually.

## "What's your relationship with insurers / BACB?"

None. I'm independent. Insurance acceptance comes from asking your front desk, not from payer
directories, because those are wrong often enough that regulators call them ghost networks. I
don't scrape the BACB registry — their terms prohibit it — so BACB certification is checked one
record at a time when a clinic claims a profile.

## "Do you sell our leads to competitors?"

No. A family's inquiry goes to the clinic that family chose, and nowhere else. I don't sell
contact data, and I don't charge per referral — flat monthly only.
